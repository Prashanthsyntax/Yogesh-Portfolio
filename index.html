Publications & Research Papers

IEEE Conference Paper

A DBN-Based Approach: Fault Detection in Transmission Lines with Z-score Technique and Adam Optimizer

Koyi Kotaiah Chowdary, R. Yogesh, Geeta Dhyani, D. Swetha Deepika, V. T. Ram Pavan Kumar M, Ch Ravi Swaroop

2025 International Conference on Emerging Research in Computational Science (ICERCS) • 2025

Abstract: Presents a Deep Belief Network (DBN)-based approach for detecting and classifying transmission line faults using voltage and current measurements. The methodology applies Z-score normalization for data preprocessing and the Adam optimizer for model training to identify different symmetrical and unsymmetrical faults. The model achieved 86.27% test accuracy and a mean AUC of 0.9787.

DOI: 10.1109/ICERCS65898.2025.11581027
View PDF DOI
IEEE Conference Paper

Detection of Phishing Websites Using Machine Learning

Mr. R. Yogesh, A. Sushma, D. Navya, K. Bhavana, V. Manasa

2025 International Conference on Computer Communication and Informatics (ICCCI) • 2025

Abstract: Explores machine learning techniques for identifying phishing websites by analyzing URL characteristics and website-related features. The study compares Logistic Regression, Multinomial Naive Bayes, and XGBoost algorithms. Logistic Regression achieved an accuracy of 96.63% in the reported comparison. The system also describes preprocessing, tokenization, Count Vectorization, and FastAPI-based deployment.

DOI: 10.1109/ICCCI54379.2022.9740763
